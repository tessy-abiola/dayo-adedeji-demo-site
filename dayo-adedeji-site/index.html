About me

Hello, I'm Dayo.

Founder & CEO of Dynasty Africa, and still Understanding Attention.

I build at the point where creativity, culture, marketing and business meet. I've spoken openly about starting Dynasty Africa without an established playbook, about learning from failure, and about what pressure and trust do to a company.

I care about developing future leaders rather than just employees, and about helping African brands understand the audiences they are speaking to, and turn that understanding into commercial results.
