About Dayo

Founder & CEO of Dynasty Africa, and still a student of the game.

Dayo builds at the point where creativity, culture, marketing and business meet. He has spoken publicly about starting Dynasty Africa without an established playbook, about learning from failure, and about what pressure and trust do to a company.

He cares about developing future leaders rather than just employees, and about helping African brands understand the audiences they are speaking to, and turn that understanding into commercial results.
